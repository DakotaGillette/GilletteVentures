# Home surface brief

Scope: `/` (home), with the shared layout reaching contact, privacy and terms. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: verifiers (banks, processors, app stores, partners) confirming the company is real and competent; action is to copy the company details or write to us.

Constraints: no venture names, counts, metrics or founding dates. Company speaks as "we".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, played straight at Linear/Stripe/Raycast craft: a dark, precise company page. It refuses the padded template version (icon feature cards, stats, process explainers): the page says what the company is in one line, states four guiding principles as plain text, and puts the verifiable facts to work.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground (#08090B) with raised panels a step lighter, hairline borders, one blue-violet accent used for live signal only. Geist throughout, tight negative tracking on display, white primary buttons, ghost secondary buttons, rounded 10–14px corners.

STORY: The visitor learns we build our own products and that the LLC is their parent company, reads four guiding principles (How we work), finds every legal detail in one copyable table, gets plain answers to common questions, and has a direct way to write to us. The principles and questions were added at the user's request with user-approved copy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned headline at display scale, one sentence beneath, primary "Contact us" and secondary "Company details" buttons, over a dot-grid light field lit by one slowly drifting accent glow at upper right.

FORM: Category canon, chosen by the user over the roll (assigned index 7, company stationery). seed: none (unavailable; the roll printed no SEED REFERENCE line, only a raw key, and the user chose the canon card)

Signature interaction: copy buttons on every company detail (for verifiers pasting into forms) confirm with an icon morph; the closing contact panel carries a pointer-following spotlight.

Motion grammar: on load, headline lines rise from 8px with blur clearing (exponential ease-out, 60ms stagger), the light field fades up and the glow drifts on an 18s loop. Reduced motion: everything static and visible.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
