# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People checking that Gillette Ventures LLC is a real company: banks, payment processors (Stripe and similar), app stores, vendors and prospective partners. They arrive from a form field, an onboarding review or a search, and need to confirm the legal name, principal address and a way to reach the company. Secondary: anyone curious about who is behind a venture.

## Product Purpose

The public home of Gillette Ventures LLC, an umbrella holding company. Each project starts inside the LLC, trades under a registered DBA, and spins out into its own company once it earns enough. Success is a verifier confirming the company in seconds and leaving with confidence that it is a competent, real operator.

## Positioning

An operator, not an agency or investor: Gillette Ventures builds and runs its own products and takes no client work. The ventures are open-ended; no fixed category.

## Operating Context

Visited mostly once, during a verification or partner check, on desktop and phone. Contact happens by email (admin@gilletteventures.com). There is deliberately no contact form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: home, contact (email and address, no form), privacy, terms, 404. All prerendered.
- Astro 7 on Cloudflare Workers; business facts live in `src/config/site.ts`.
- Undecided: state of formation. Ventures are deliberately not listed yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Gillette Ventures; legal name Gillette Ventures LLC. No logo yet.
- Voice: the company speaking as "we". Plain and factual. No founder name on the site.
- Visual standing preference: the category standard (a dark, minimal tech-company site) executed at full craft. Quality bar: Linear, Stripe and Raycast.

## Evidence on Hand

No venture list, customers, metrics, testimonials or press. Do not invent any of them, including venture names, counts or founding dates.

## Product Principles

- Verifiable first: legal name, address and contact are always one glance away.
- Say what is true, once. No hype and no implied services for hire.
- Do not explain the internal structure (DBAs, shared banking, spin-out plans) on the site; one framing line that it is the parent company is enough.
- The site itself is the proof of competence: its craft stands in for a portfolio that is not public yet.
